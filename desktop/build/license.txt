Tablewise is a database manager for people who do not write SQL.

By installing Tablewise you agree that:
- You are responsible for the databases you connect to and the changes you make.
- Always keep backups of important data. Tablewise asks before deleting, but server databases cannot be undone.
- AI features send your question and your table structure to the AI provider you choose. Example rows are only sent if you turn on "Let the AI look at data".

Built with Electron, sql.js, node-postgres, mysql2, mssql and SheetJS, each under its own open-source license.
